## 5. Bull Case vs Bear Case

### Bull Case
- **Google deal confirmed + new hyperscaler wins**: If Alphabet signs, Marvell ends Broadcom's monopoly on Google's most important AI silicon program. ASIC contracts are 3–5 year relationships worth billions annually. A single confirmation announcement would likely gap the stock above $180.
- **FY2027 beat-and-raise cadence**: Management guided $11B for FY2027 (+30% YoY). Every quarter of FY2026 beat estimates. If AI capex holds at $720B+/year, underdelivering on AI demand seems unlikely. Analysts will chase the stock higher.
- **ASIC > GPU structural shift**: TrendForce projects 45% ASIC market growth in 2026 vs 16% GPU growth. Marvell at #2 in custom silicon with 15% share and accelerating is the cleanest pure-play on this trend.
- **Nvidia ecosystem lock-in**: The $2B preferred investment is not passive — it signals deep co-development commitments that compound into future design wins.
- **UBS $195 target credible**: At ~18x FY2027E revenue (~$11B), $195 is consistent with high-growth semiconductor valuations. If Google deal materializes, $200+ enters the conversation.

### Bear Case
- **Stock is 35–40% above analyst consensus**: The median Street PT is $119–$122. MRVL at $168 has already priced in 12–18 months of further upgrades. Mean reversion to consensus would be a 25–30% drawdown.
- **Google deal is rumor, not contract**: The Alphabet collaboration was reported by The Information — not confirmed by either company. If it's delayed, reduced in scope, or falls through, the primary April catalyst evaporates.
- **Insider exodus with no buyer**: CEO, COO, and CLO all sold shares in March-April 2026. Not a single insider buy in 6+ months. The people with the deepest information advantage are reducing exposure.
- **Broadcom's structural lead is vast**: AVGO holds ~70% of the custom AI accelerator market vs MRVL's 15%. Entrenched multi-year TPU/MTIA programs are extremely hard to displace. Marvell's wins are still incremental.
- **Valuation + beta = painful drawdowns**: At 55x P/E and 1.69 beta, a soft macro signal, China/Taiwan escalation, or missed guidance quarter would hit MRVL far harder than the broader market.
- **2019 SEC revenue manipulation precedent**: While settled, it signals cultural willingness to manage revenue aggressively to meet expectations — a watch item for any future guidance pressure.
