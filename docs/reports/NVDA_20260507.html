5. Bull Case vs Bear Case

Bull Case

  • Q1 FY2027 beat-and-raise is overwhelmingly likely: MSFT and META just lifted 2026 capex by $36B and $10B respectively. Hyperscaler capex commitments imply NVDA underdelivers on demand, not the other way around. $78B guidance midpoint should be raised to $80B+.
  • Forward P/E ~26x is cheap for 50%+ growth: The multiple has compressed despite earnings power expanding. NVDA at 26x forward earnings is trading at a *discount* to MRVL (42x) and AAOI/CRDO/AXTI on growth-adjusted basis.
  • 80%+ AI accelerator share intact: Custom ASICs from AVGO/MRVL are growing fast in *absolute* terms but not stealing share from NVDA's GPU sockets — they address different workloads. NVDA still defines the high-end training/inference market.
  • $96.6B FCF + $54B net cash = capital return runway: Buyback acceleration is increasingly likely as the company hits cash limits. Each $50B in buybacks at current price retires ~1% of shares.
  • AI inference scaling = secular GPU demand: Inference workloads scale with model usage, not just training. Every new ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini query consumes NVDA silicon.
  • Zero Wall Street downgrades, average PT $270: 36% upside to consensus on a name with cleanest fundamentals — extremely rare setup.

Bear Case

  • Revenue growth deceleration is real: 114% (FY25) → 65% (FY26) → projected ~48% (FY27). The growth rate has halved in 18 months. Multiple expansion is unlikely from here.
  • Insider sells dwarf any signal: Jensen Huang sold $1B+ in calendar 2025 alone; collective insider sales >$3.3B over 18 months with zero buys. Insiders distribute when they think the multiple has peaked, not bottomed.
  • China is structurally lost revenue: H20 ban is "indefinite"; China antitrust investigation ongoing (Mellanox-related); $5.5B charge already taken. China revenue line is now zero in forward guidance — that revenue is gone unless export rules fully reverse.
  • Custom ASIC / hyperscaler vertical integration: Google TPU programs (via AVGO+MRVL), Amazon Trainium, Meta MTIA — these reduce hyperscaler reliance on NVDA over time. Pricing power erodes as alternatives mature.
  • Securities lawsuit (cert granted): Class action over crypto miner revenue disclosures from 2017-2018 is moving forward against NVDA and Huang personally — meaningful tail risk.
  • Mega-cap math is unforgiving: At $4.77T market cap, NVDA needs to add ~$1.7T in market cap to hit consensus PT. That's larger than the entire market cap of every company except MSFT and AAPL. Mega-cap mean-reversion gravity is real.
  • 2026 "consolidation year" thesis: Several analysts argue 2026 is more about digestion than expansion after FY25's 114% growth.
